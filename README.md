# NSAP – Social Protection and Pension Coverage Analysis

This project examines the distribution of old-age, widow and disability pensions under India's National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP). It uses government datasets, Python-based data analysis, visualisation and K-means clustering to explore differences in pension beneficiary distribution and their relationship with multidimensional poverty across Indian States and Union Territories.

The study aims to understand patterns in social protection and highlight potential gaps in reaching vulnerable populations.
